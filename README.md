# www.diego-appraiser.com

City of San Diego site of Brian Ward Appraisal (parent site: https://www.brianward.com).
Static site deployed to Cloudflare Pages project `diego-appraiser-com` (custom domains diego-appraiser.com and www.diego-appraiser.com).

- 161 pages: home, 12 service pages, pricing, 64 City of San Diego neighborhood pages, 63 San Diego County community pages, 12 district hubs, FAQ, about, process, reviews, contact
- Images generated with Google Gemini (img/, thumbnails in img/t/)
- Contact form posts to https://www.brianward.com/api/contact
