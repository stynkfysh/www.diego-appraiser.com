About Brian Ward

A California certified residential appraiser who has worked in San Diego since 2004.

Brian Ward holds California's Certified Residential appraiser credential and has valued San Diego homes since 2004. Over more than twenty years and upward of 7,000 assignments, his practice has moved almost entirely to work that has nothing to do with mortgage lending: probate and trust administration, marital dissolution, bankruptcy, litigation, tax matters and private decisions about buying, selling or dividing a family home.

That focus matters because the questions are different. A lender wants to know whether a loan is safe. An executor needs a number the IRS will accept as of a specific date. A family-law attorney needs a report that will hold up when the other side hires its own appraiser. A trustee needs to divide a house fairly among siblings who may not agree. Each of those reports is written for the people who will actually rely on it.

diego-appraiser.com is the City of San Diego site of Brian Ward Appraisal. His primary website is brianward.com, which reaches across both San Diego and Riverside Counties. Whichever site you start from, you are working with the same appraiser, the same fees and the same office.
