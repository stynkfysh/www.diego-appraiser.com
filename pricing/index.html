What moves a quote

The starting prices fit an ordinary house on an ordinary lot. A La Jolla view home, a Rancho Peñasquitos property with an unpermitted addition, a Point Loma lot split into two parcels, or a retrospective date that needs older sales data all take more time and are priced accordingly. Rush delivery for a hearing or filing date is quoted separately.

Matching the report to the purpose

Desktop reports are built from records and work well when the parties agree on condition — many trust distributions and sibling buyouts fall here. A drive-by adds an exterior viewing. A standard report includes a walk-through and is the better fit when someone may challenge the number, when the house has been heavily remodeled or neglected, or when a judge is likely to read it.

Testimony and consulting

Depositions, trial appearances and review of another appraiser's work are billed separately from the report. Ask for a quote when you order if testimony is likely.

Paying for the report

The report is paid for on delivery — card, check or transfer. Trustees and law firms with several properties can request invoicing.
