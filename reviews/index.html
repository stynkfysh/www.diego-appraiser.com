Reviews and references

How to check Brian Ward's work — and how to share your own experience.

This site does not republish testimonials. Instead, here is how to verify the appraiser and read what past clients have said in places that are independently managed.

Verify the license

California appraiser licenses can be looked up on the Bureau of Real Estate Appraisers website by name. Brian Ward holds a Certified Residential credential and has been appraising since 2004.

Read reviews

Client reviews for Brian Ward Appraisal are collected on the main practice website and on Google. Visit brianward.com to find current links.

Leave a review

If Brian Ward appraised a property for you, a short review describing the purpose of the report and how the process went helps other San Diego families and attorneys decide whom to call. Feedback, positive or not, can also be sent directly to contact@diego-appraiser.com.

References for attorneys and fiduciaries

Law offices and professional fiduciaries considering a first assignment can ask for a sample redacted report and a description of past testimony experience.
