Questions about San Diego appraisals

Straight answers on fees, timing, report types and what to expect.

How much does a home appraisal cost in San Diego?

Fees start at $299 for a records-only desktop valuation and top out at a $799 starting price for walking through a duplex, triplex or fourplex. A typical single-family home with an interior inspection starts at $699. The written quote you receive before booking is the price you pay unless the scope changes.

Is this the same kind of appraisal a bank orders?

The analysis is similar, but these reports are ordered directly by you or your attorney rather than by a lender, so they can be written for estate, court, tax or private decision-making purposes. Lenders generally will not accept a report they did not order through their own channels.

Can you value a home as of a past date?

Yes. Retrospective appraisals are routine for date-of-death, separation-date and bankruptcy-petition-date work. The comparable sales are drawn from the period around the effective date, and the report describes the property as it existed then.

How soon can the report be finished?

A typical San Diego house report goes out a few business days after the site visit, or after the property details arrive for a desktop assignment. If a hearing or filing date is driving the timeline, say so in the request and the schedule is built around it.

Do I need to be there for the inspection?

Someone with access needs to open the home, but it does not have to be the person who ordered the report. Executors often send a relative, agent or property manager. Drive-by and desktop reports need no access at all.

Which report type should I choose?

When everyone agrees on the home's condition, a desktop report is often enough. When the value may be challenged — a contested divorce, litigation, a bankruptcy objection — a standard report with an interior inspection gives the strongest support. If you are unsure, choose 'Not sure' on the request form.

Do you appraise condos and multi-unit buildings?

Yes. Downtown and Mission Valley condominiums, Pacific Beach duplexes, North Park fourplexes and townhomes throughout the city are all regular assignments. Two- to four-unit buildings have their own fee tier.

Will you testify about the appraisal?

Brian Ward can provide deposition and trial testimony on reports he prepared. Testimony and preparation time are billed separately from the report itself and quoted in advance.

Do you cover areas outside the City of San Diego?

Yes. While this site focuses on the city's neighborhoods, the same service covers every city and unincorporated community in San Diego County, from Oceanside and Fallbrook to Borrego Springs and Imperial Beach.

What should I have ready before ordering?

The address, why the value is needed, the valuation date if it is not today, the names of any attorneys involved, and notes on recent improvements or known problems such as unpermitted additions or deferred maintenance.

How is payment handled?

The fee is paid when the finished report is sent, by credit card, check or bank transfer. Law firms and trustees ordering multiple reports can arrange invoice billing.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Appraisal assignments are confidential under the professional standards that govern California appraisers. Reports are released only to the client and the intended users named in the engagement.
