Why local knowledge matters

What a San Diego appraisal has to account for

Canyons change the math

A house on a canyon rim in University Heights and one two blocks inland can share a ZIP code and still sell tens of thousands of dollars apart. Slope, usable yard, brush-management setbacks and the view across the canyon all show up in the comparable sales, and Brian Ward measures those differences instead of averaging them away.

The coast is its own market

West of Interstate 5 the rules shift: the 30-foot coastal height limit, Coastal Commission permitting, short-term rental licensing and salt-air wear on older construction. Beach-area reports lean on sales from the same side of the freeway and the same distance to the sand.

Records first, then the site visit

Every assignment starts with permits, county assessor data, prior MLS history and the sales most like your property. When the report includes an inspection, the visit confirms what the records say and catches what they miss — an unpermitted garage conversion, a new roof, a cracked slab.

Date of Death Appraisals

A retrospective appraisal that pins down what a San Diego house was worth on the date its owner died, for stepped-up basis, estate filings and fair division among heirs.

Estate & Trust Appraisals

Independent valuations of San Diego homes held in an estate or living trust, prepared for trustees, heirs and advisors who must divide, distribute, sell or account for real property.

Divorce Appraisals

Neutral, court-ready valuations of a San Diego marital home or rental property, prepared for spouses, attorneys and mediators working out a property division or buyout.

Bankruptcy Appraisals

Supported market values for San Diego homes and small rental buildings in Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases, written for debtors, attorneys and trustees reviewing scheduled equity.

Expert Witness & Litigation Support

Real estate valuation opinions, report reviews and courtroom testimony from a California certified residential appraiser, for San Diego attorneys handling property-related disputes and lawsuits.

Tax Appraisals

Appraisals for property tax appeals, gift tax filings, charitable donations and capital gains basis questions, covering San Diego homes, condos and small rental buildings.

Pre-Purchase Appraisals

An independent value opinion for San Diego buyers who want to know what a home is really worth before they write an offer or remove their appraisal contingency.

Pre-Sale Appraisals

An independent market value for San Diego owners preparing to sell, so pricing, repair choices and buyer appraisal risks are understood before the home goes on the market.

Family Transaction Appraisals

Independent appraisals for San Diego homes transferred, sold or shared among relatives, supporting fair pricing, gift reporting, Proposition 19 questions and buyouts.

Insurance Dispute Appraisals

How an assignment runs

  1. Tell us the property and the purpose

    Send the address, the reason for the appraisal and any deadline. For date-of-death or retrospective work, include the effective date.

  2. Get a written quote

    You receive the report type that fits, a firm fee and a delivery date before anything is scheduled. Nothing is booked until you approve.

  3. Inspection or records review

    Standard and drive-by assignments include a scheduled visit; desktop reports are completed from records, photos and market data.

  4. Report delivered as a PDF

    A signed narrative report with the comparable sales, adjustments and reasoning, ready for an attorney, court, trustee or the IRS.

Common questions

How much does a home appraisal cost in San Diego?

Fees start at $299 for a records-only desktop valuation and top out at a $799 starting price for walking through a duplex, triplex or fourplex. A typical single-family home with an interior inspection starts at $699. The written quote you receive before booking is the price you pay unless the scope changes.

Is this the same kind of appraisal a bank orders?

The analysis is similar, but these reports are ordered directly by you or your attorney rather than by a lender, so they can be written for estate, court, tax or private decision-making purposes. Lenders generally will not accept a report they did not order through their own channels.

Can you value a home as of a past date?

Yes. Retrospective appraisals are routine for date-of-death, separation-date and bankruptcy-petition-date work. The comparable sales are drawn from the period around the effective date, and the report describes the property as it existed then.

How soon can the report be finished?

A typical San Diego house report goes out a few business days after the site visit, or after the property details arrive for a desktop assignment. If a hearing or filing date is driving the timeline, say so in the request and the schedule is built around it.

Do I need to be there for the inspection?

Someone with access needs to open the home, but it does not have to be the person who ordered the report. Executors often send a relative, agent or property manager. Drive-by and desktop reports need no access at all.
