Appraisal services in San Diego

Twelve kinds of non-lending residential appraisals, each written for the people who will rely on it.

Date of Death Appraisals

A retrospective appraisal that pins down what a San Diego house was worth on the date its owner died, for stepped-up basis, estate filings and fair division among heirs.

Estate & Trust Appraisals

Independent valuations of San Diego homes held in an estate or living trust, prepared for trustees, heirs and advisors who must divide, distribute, sell or account for real property.

Divorce Appraisals

Neutral, court-ready valuations of a San Diego marital home or rental property, prepared for spouses, attorneys and mediators working out a property division or buyout.

Bankruptcy Appraisals

Supported market values for San Diego homes and small rental buildings in Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases, written for debtors, attorneys and trustees reviewing scheduled equity.

Expert Witness & Litigation Support

Real estate valuation opinions, report reviews and courtroom testimony from a California certified residential appraiser, for San Diego attorneys handling property-related disputes and lawsuits.

Tax Appraisals

Appraisals for property tax appeals, gift tax filings, charitable donations and capital gains basis questions, covering San Diego homes, condos and small rental buildings.

Pre-Purchase Appraisals

An independent value opinion for San Diego buyers who want to know what a home is really worth before they write an offer or remove their appraisal contingency.

Pre-Sale Appraisals

An independent market value for San Diego owners preparing to sell, so pricing, repair choices and buyer appraisal risks are understood before the home goes on the market.

Family Transaction Appraisals

Independent appraisals for San Diego homes transferred, sold or shared among relatives, supporting fair pricing, gift reporting, Proposition 19 questions and buyouts.

Insurance Dispute Appraisals

Before-and-after market value analysis for San Diego homeowners, adjusters and attorneys sorting out real estate losses from fire, water, flood, slope movement or other covered events.

Immigration Appraisals

Documented market values for San Diego real estate used as financial evidence in immigration matters, including sponsor asset declarations and proof of funds requirements.

Bail Bond Appraisals

Fast, supported valuations of San Diego real estate offered as collateral for bail, prepared so bondsmen, courts and families can confirm equity quickly.
