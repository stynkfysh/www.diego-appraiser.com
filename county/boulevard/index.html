Boulevard home appraisals

Wind-swept high desert hamlet near the border

Boulevard is a high desert community along Old Highway 80 and Interstate 8 near the Imperial County line. Large parcels, older homes and energy projects shape the area, and comparable sales require wide searches and careful adjustment.

The Boulevard market

Boulevard sits at about 3,500 feet in the far southeastern corner of San Diego County. It includes the small village core along Old Highway 80, along with scattered settlements such as Live Oak Springs, Tierra del Sol and Manzanita. Wind energy projects on the Campo Reservation and in McCain Valley are visible on the ridgelines. The area is known for open land, rocky terrain and expansive views. Residents include retirees, ranchers and people seeking low-density living.

Homes and housing stock

Housing consists of older cabins, ranch homes and manufactured homes on acreage, with a scattering of newer custom homes. Many parcels range from a few acres to forty acres or more, and boulder-strewn terrain often limits building sites. Wells and septic systems are standard, and some properties are fully off grid. Outbuildings, water storage tanks, fencing and solar arrays are common improvements, and dirt roads serve many parcels.

What moves values in Boulevard

Energy infrastructure

Wind turbines and transmission lines may affect views and buyer perception. Brian Ward considers proximity to these features.

Remote location

Distance from employment centers narrows demand and affects marketing time.

Off-grid properties

Solar systems, generators and water storage vary widely in quality and contribution to value.

Limited sales

Comparable searches may extend to Jacumba, Campo and other rural areas with adjustments for location and land.

Appraisal assignments here

Much of the land around Boulevard was bought decades ago by owners now in their later years, so trust and probate appraisals are frequent. Divorce valuations of rural acreage and property tax appeals also come up. Buyers drawn by lower prices often request pre-purchase appraisals before committing to a high desert parcel with a well, septic and off-grid power. Insurance claims after wind or fire damage occasionally call for independent valuation as well.
