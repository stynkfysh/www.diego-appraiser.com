Imperial Beach home appraisals

Southernmost beach town between bay, estuary and border

The Imperial Beach market

Imperial Beach holds the distinction of being the most southerly beach city in the continental United States, bounded by the Pacific Ocean, the south end of San Diego Bay and the Tijuana River estuary along the Mexican border. It has a pier, a long sand beach and a bayfront path on the Bayshore Bikeway. Cross-border sewage flows from the Tijuana River have caused frequent beach closures for years. The city has added newer hotels and mixed-use buildings near the pier, while most neighborhoods remain modest postwar housing.

Homes and housing stock

Housing includes small postwar single-family homes, duplexes and fourplexes on narrow lots, older condominium buildings and newer beachfront projects along Seacoast Drive. Many inland homes are one-story stucco or frame houses from the 1950s and 1960s, some expanded with additions. Lots are typically small and flat. Rentals make up a large share of the stock, and attached product is common near the beach.

What moves values in Imperial Beach

Water quality closures

Ongoing beach closures from the Tijuana River can weigh on demand for oceanfront and near-beach property.

Flood and sea-level risk

Low-lying blocks near the estuary and the ocean face flooding and future sea-level rise concerns.

Helicopter training noise

Naval Outlying Landing Field Imperial Beach generates frequent helicopter activity that affects nearby homes.

Small multifamily

Duplexes and fourplexes may be priced on rental income as well as on comparable sales.

Appraisal assignments here

Estate and trust appraisals are common in Imperial Beach because many homes have been owned since the 1960s and 1970s. Divorce appraisals often involve small rental properties or beach condominiums. Pre-sale appraisals help owners price homes in a market where water-quality news can shift demand, and Brian Ward also prepares values for family transfers and partition disputes involving inherited duplexes.
